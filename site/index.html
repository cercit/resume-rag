        Your file stays on your device

        PDF and DOCX files are converted to text in your browser. You can review that text before generating.

        Text is sent to an AI service

        Your resume text and job description are sent through our server to OpenRouter to prepare the rewrite. Free model providers may log prompts. Remove information you don't want processed before you continue.

        Sessions expire after 7 days

        Resume Tailor's backend removes session data after 7 days. Sign-in is not remembered in your browser: you enter a new email code on each visit.
