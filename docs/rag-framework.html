How the resume tailor works

A visitor gives their resume and a job description. The tool looks up writing advice from your notes, asks an AI to rewrite the resume for that job, checks the result, and prints it in your 2-page format.

The idea in three parts

What RAG means here

RetrieveFind the 8 most relevant passages from your 9 career documents, plus 3 bullet-writing tips, by meaning, not keywords.
AugmentPut those passages into the AI's instructions, clearly labelled as writing advice and never as facts about the person.
GenerateThe AI writes the tailored resume as structured data. Every fact comes from the visitor's own resume text.
Overview

The whole flow

Part 1, run once

Loading your notes into the database

  1. Read the documents

    your PC

    Markdown and text are read as they are. PDFs are turned into text. Images and image folders are skipped, and image links are removed.

  2. Cut into chunks

    your PC

    Split at headings, then any long section is cut at about 2,800 characters with 300 characters of overlap. Each chunk keeps its file title and heading. Reference lists are dropped.

  3. Tag a topic

    your PC

    bullets for the XYZ formula and examples, ats for parsing tips, research for hiring reports and papers, general for the rest.

  4. Turn each chunk into numbers

    your PC

    The gte-small model gives each chunk a list of 384 numbers that captures its meaning. Similar ideas get similar numbers.

  5. Store and verify

    Supabase

    Chunks go into the documents table. A check embeds one chunk again on the server and confirms the database finds that same chunk (similarity 0.9999999).

Part 2, every visitor

Making one tailored resume

  1. Sign in with an email code

    browser

    No passwords. Everything after this needs a signed-in user.

  2. Resume becomes text

    browser

    The browser reads the PDF or DOCX and shows the text so the person can fix it. A scanned file with no text asks them to paste it instead. Files never leave the browser; only text is sent.

  3. Check the daily cap first

    generate

    3 sessions per person per day. Checked before any AI call, so a blocked person costs nothing.

  4. Pull out the facts

    AI, fast model

    The AI turns the resume text into a profile: jobs, titles, dates, bullets, tools, education. Only what is written, nothing inferred.

  5. Find relevant advice

    match_documents

    The job description is turned into the same kind of numbers. The database returns the 8 closest chunks from your notes, plus the 3 closest bullet-writing tips.

  6. Write the tailored resume

    AI, strong model

    The AI gets the profile, the job description, the advice and your /human writing rules. It reorders and rewords bullets to match the job, and returns JSON in your template's shape.

  7. Check and save

    generate

    Guards check the result (next section). It is saved as version 1 of the session.

  8. Print in your format

    browser

    render.js is a line-by-line port of your build_resume.py. It tightens spacing in 5 steps until the resume fits 2 A4 pages, then the person saves it as a PDF.

Safety

Guards that run in code, not just in the prompt

No invented factsEvery number, employer, title, tool and seniority word must appear in the visitor's own resume text.
Locked fieldsName, contact, education, certifications and job titles are copied from the profile, never from the AI.
Plain writingBanned words such as "leveraging" or "spearheaded" are caught. Odd hyphens and curly quotes are cleaned.
Valid shapeThe JSON is checked against a schema. One retry lists every problem; after that, bad lines are removed.
Notes are advice onlyRetrieved chunks are labelled "writing guidance, not facts about the candidate".
Text onlyThe AI never receives a file or image. Instructions hidden in a resume or job description are treated as text.
After the first draft

Tweaks and limits

A person can ask for changes like "shorten the summary" or "put the Python project first". The AI changes only the parts that need it, the same guards run, and a new version is saved. Undo goes back one version.
